### What Round 1 asked teams to articulate

The 2025 deck's Lean Canvas included: [S3]

- The top 1–3 problems and existing alternatives.
- A proposed solution for each problem.
- A clear unique value proposition and high-level concept.
- Target customers/users and channels for reaching them.
- Key metrics and an advantage that is difficult to copy.
- College name, team name, and team members.

**Preparation implication, not a 2026 requirement:** Have a crisp explanation of the problem, intended users, proposed solution, and measurable benefit ready before spending heavily on implementation.

The 2025 SlideShare deck lists: [S3]

- **Usability and scalability:** intuitive interface, ease of use, and ability to scale.

**Document inconsistency:** Its innovation wording mentions “enhancing customer experience,” although the displayed problem concerns campus recruitment. That may be generic or reused wording. Do not treat it as a complete or precise judging rubric, and do not infer scoring weights.

## 5. Suggested approach — advice, not official rules

1. **Capture the current brief first.** Identify the exact problem, mandatory features, deliverables, and deadline for the next round.
2. **Pick one demonstrable workflow.** For example, for a recruitment challenge: import profiles → search → match a role → explain a shortlist. Avoid building a large platform before the core works.
3. **Define a measurable benefit.** Examples: reduced shortlisting effort, better matching relevance, or improved accessibility. Distinguish measured results from aspirations.
4. **Build an end-to-end prototype with safe demo data.** Do not assume access to real Citi systems or personal financial/student data. Confirm permitted datasets and external services.
5. **Prepare the explanation alongside the code.** State the user problem, existing alternative, distinctive contribution, architecture, limitations, and demo outcome.
6. **Only use AI where it earns its place.** Choose technology based on the actual brief and permitted tools, not the fact that older teams used it.
