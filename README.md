# Citi India Hackathon 2026 — Ada Lovelace

## Round 1 proposal

**Concept:** A handy, personalized career counsellor that understands you and grows with you.

The proposed platform connects career exploration, learning pathways, and livelihood opportunities for rural school and college students. Initial testing is planned with Classes 11–12 in rural government schools, with college testing to follow as access and capacity develop. Institutional permissions and participant consent are required; no pilot or partnership is confirmed.
